# INDRISMA — Music, Out of This World

An ambient listening room. Endless shuffled channels of music over living, breathing
scenery — rain with a couple under one umbrella, nebulae, rivers, glaciers — plus a
full-screen real-time player and a film watchlist that follows you across devices
with one 5-letter key.

## What's inside

- **Shuffle-only radio** — six channels of YouTube playlists, infinitely shuffled.
  No next/previous: the mix draws its own cards. Paste any YouTube video or
  playlist link to take over.
- **Living scenes** — seven ambient backdrops with particles, horizon glow, and a
  slow lightning pulse in Rain, where two silhouettes kiss under one umbrella.
- **Real-time player (V)** — the full-screen stage. The actual video becomes
  visible with the aurora visualizer bleeding over its bottom edge, seekable
  progress whenever the stream has a duration, and a LIVE badge for 24/7 radios.
- **Films** — Discover shows upcoming movies and on-air series from TMDB; search
  films and series, build a watchlist, mark watched, rate.
- **5-letter sync key** — create a key like `K7XQF`, enter it on any other device,
  and your lists follow you. Legacy `vrx-…` keys from the old build still work.
- **Keyboard** — Space/P play-pause · M mute · ←/→ seek ±10s (videos) · V video
  stage · F films · ? shortcuts · Esc close.
